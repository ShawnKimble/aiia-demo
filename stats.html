What we track — in the open

We sell independent verification of AI systems. Verification starts with showing your own work — so here is our entire tracking footprint, and our live visitor numbers.
✓ No cookies ✓ No visitor IDs ✓ Counts published live

What we collect

  • Anonymous visit counts per page, via GoatCounter (open-source analytics)
  • Aggregate country, browser, and screen-size totals — numbers like "21 visits from the US," never a record of a person
  • Referrer, when your browser sends one (links from chat apps usually don't)
  • Field stories you choose to submit — delivered via Formspree, which logs standard request metadata (like country) as any form processor does. Only what you deliberately send; leave the contact field blank to stay anonymous to us

What we never collect

  • Cookies, fingerprints, or any identifier that could recognize you tomorrow
  • Your risk-triage selections — the checklist runs entirely in your browser and transmits nothing
  • Names, emails, or accounts — there is nothing to sign up for, and a story submission carries only the contact you choose to include

Because we set no identifiers, we genuinely cannot tell a new visitor from a returning one. Most analytics vendors treat that as a limitation. We treat it as the spec.
