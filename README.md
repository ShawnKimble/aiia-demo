## About AI·IA

The overlap between IT and Finance grows every day, and AI agents now operate inside ERPs, close platforms, and expense systems under trusted identities at machine speed. Access reports tell you who *could* act; AI·IA focuses on what an AI will actually *do* — pressure-testing AI the way attackers and auditors actually think.

The triage is informational only — not an audit, security assessment, legal advice, or professional opinion.
